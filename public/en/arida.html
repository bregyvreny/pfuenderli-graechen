Granary ARIDA PRATA – pure nature

The natural way to slow down: a centuries-old granary in the middle of a flower meadow, overlooking the peaks of the Matter Valley. Deliberately without internet or TV – here you cook over the fire, hot water comes from a wood-fired boiler, and in the evening you sit at the big table by the barbecue. With a bit of luck, an ibex will even drop by – they are frequent visitors here.

  • Kitchen with wood-fired stove

  • Shower/WC against the rock wall
  • Balcony with mountain views, loungers in the meadow
  • Large table with barbecue
  • Deliberately without Wi-Fi and TV
  • Ibex as frequent neighbours

Getting here

Parking on the road at Haus Mustang. From there it is about 300 m on foot to the granary – luggage has to be carried. Tip: travel light or bring a backpack.
