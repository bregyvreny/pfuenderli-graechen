Chalet EFEU – with style & tradition

A traditional chalet in the car-free old part of Grächen, just 200 m from the village centre. From the balcony you look out onto the Brunegghorn and Weisshorn – and fresh bread from z'Pfünderli is waiting right next door.

Amenities

  • 2 double rooms and 2 single rooms (2 with washbasin)
  • Kitchen-living room with dining area
  • Shower/WC
  • Balcony with mountain views
  • Flat-screen TV, free Wi-Fi
  • Dishwasher, Nespresso machine
  • Fondue and raclette set
  • Sauna in the house (extra charge)

Getting here

Both are in the car-free part of Grächen. Please call us about 30 minutes before you arrive (+41 79 331 61 47). We meet at the village square next to the tourist office, where there are short-term parking spaces to wait. We pick you up with our electric cart, show you the car park in the village centre and take you and your luggage to the apartment.
