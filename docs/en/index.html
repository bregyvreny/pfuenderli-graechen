Location & arrival

In the heart of old Grächen

Our apartments are in the car-free, historic part of the village – quiet, yet only a few steps from shops, restaurants and the cable car.

Getting here

By train to St. Niklaus, then by PostBus to Grächen.

  • ARIDA PRATA: parking on the road at Haus Mustang, then a walk of about 300 m. Luggage has to be carried.
  • EFEU & EGGERI: Both are in the car-free part of Grächen. Please call us about 30 minutes before you arrive (+41 79 331 61 47). We meet at the village square next to the tourist office, where there are short-term parking spaces to wait. We pick you up with our electric cart, show you the car park in the village centre and take you and your luggage to the apartment.

Prices · Payment · Cancellation

Fair and simple

Price

One flat daily rate for the whole apartment – everything included, regardless of the number of guests. No further costs on site.

Payment

On site in cash or by credit card, or in advance by bank transfer.

Cancellation

Free of charge up to 14 days before arrival. For later cancellations, we charge one night.

Sauna

In Haus EFEU, for guests of EFEU and EGGERI. Choose between a steam sauna (55 °C) or a Finnish sauna (approx. 85 °C). CHF 30 base fee plus CHF 15 per person – water, tea and towels included. Please book about 1 hour in advance.
