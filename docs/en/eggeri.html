Apartment EGGERI – the sporty one

Right at the top of Haus Silberblick with no lift – hence “the sporty one”. The stairs are worth it: from the balcony you look out onto the Bietschhorn and the old village centre. In the car-free part of the village, 200 m from the centre. An ideal base for trips to Zermatt, Leukerbad, Mund or Brig.

Amenities

  • 2 double rooms (north and south)
  • Large living room with dining area
  • Small kitchen, shower/WC
  • Balcony with mountain views
  • Flat-screen TV, free Wi-Fi
  • Oven, microwave, dishwasher
  • Nespresso, fondue and raclette set
  • Sauna in Haus EFEU (extra charge)

Getting here

Both are in the car-free part of Grächen. Please call us about 30 minutes before you arrive (+41 79 331 61 47). We meet at the village square next to the tourist office, where there are short-term parking spaces to wait. We pick you up with our electric cart, show you the car park in the village centre and take you and your luggage to the apartment.
